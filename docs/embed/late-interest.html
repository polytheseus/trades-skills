Homeowners aren't covered by the late payment law, so there's no statutory interest or compensation. The calculator shows the 8% a year a county court claim in England or Wales usually adds instead.

Use the Bank Rate box only if your debt went overdue before 2023 or after 31 December 2026, or to check another figure. If your contract sets its own interest rate, use that instead: the statutory rate doesn't apply.
