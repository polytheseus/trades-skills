How the dates are read: the due date is the same date 12 months after the check (a check on 29 February is due on 28 February). The early window is the 2 months ending with the due date, so for a 15 March due date it opens on 16 January. HSE's leaflet describes it as "10 to 12 calendar months after the previous check". If your engineer prints a different expiry date, go by the earlier of the two.
